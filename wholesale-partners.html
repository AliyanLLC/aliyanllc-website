Wholesale Partners

We work with brand owners, manufacturers and authorized distributors looking for a reliable, brand-safe reseller on Amazon.com.

Become a wholesale partnerwholesale@aliyanllc.com
Who we work with

We work with businesses seeking reliable wholesale relationships.

ALIYAN LLC buys wholesale and sells to end customers on Amazon.com in the United States. We look for suppliers who want their products represented carefully and consistently.

Brand owners

Brands that want a reseller who protects pricing, keeps listings accurate and sells only authentic stock.

Manufacturers

Manufacturers with established brands that sell to approved retailers and online resellers in the US.

Authorized distributors

Distributors authorized by the brand to supply resellers, with clear account requirements and reseller policies.

The process

From first email to first order.

Opening an account follows your process. Every account is opened on your terms and subject to your approval.

01

Submit an inquiry

Tell us about your brand or the lines you distribute, your account requirements and any MAP or reseller policy.

02

Product & account review

We review the products against our categories and your requirements, and complete your new-account or credit application.

03

Wholesale discussion

We agree suitable products, opening quantities at your minimum order, payment terms and shipping instructions.

04

Partnership

Approved accounts move forward under the agreed wholesale terms: an opening purchase order, a listing check, then reorders from sell-through.

Reseller charter

Our written commitments to every brand and distributor we buy from.

I

Minimum advertised price

We never advertise below your MAP and we do not undercut the Buy Box to win sales.

II

Authorized sourcing

We buy only from brand owners and their authorized distributors, and keep every invoice on file.

III

No diversion

We sell only to end customers on Amazon.com in the United States. We do not resell to other retailers or wholesalers, and we do not export your products.

IV

Listing integrity

We sell on your existing listings and never create duplicates or change detail pages without your approval.

V

Product condition

Every unit is new, sealed, in original packaging and prepared to Amazon's FBA requirements.

VI

Your instructions

If you ask us to change how we sell your products, or to stop selling them, we act promptly.

VII

Account health

We protect our seller metrics so your products stay available and well presented.

VIII

Communication

One point of contact, and we aim to reply within one business day.

Value for your brand

More than a buyer. A caretaker for your listings.

A wholesale account should be good for the brand as well as the reseller. On the products we carry, this is the work we do for you, at no cost to you.

Listing quality

Listing review and suggestions

We review titles, images, bullet points and search terms on your listings and send you suggested improvements for your approval.

Brand Registry

A+ Content support

If you own your Brand Registry, we can draft A+ Content and Brand Store copy for you to review and publish.

Visibility

Advertising on your listings

We can run Sponsored Products campaigns on the listings we sell, at our own cost, to support your visibility on Amazon.

Protection

Listing watch

We watch your listings for counterfeit offers, listing hijacks and unauthorized sellers, and report what we find to you.

Availability

Inventory planning

We plan reorders from sell-through and ahead of peak periods such as Prime Day and the fourth quarter.

Reporting

Performance updates

Units sold, stock on hand and customer feedback for your line, shared monthly or whenever you ask.

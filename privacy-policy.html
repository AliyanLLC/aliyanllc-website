Privacy Policy

How ALIYAN LLC handles information on aliyanllc.com and in business correspondence.

Who we are

This website, aliyanllc.com, is operated by ALIYAN LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Virginia, USA. Principal business address: 209 River Bend Rd, Great Falls, VA 22066, USA. Contact: info@aliyanllc.com.

What this website collects

This is an informational website. It does not use accounts, does not sell anything online, and does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers.

Inquiry form

The inquiry form on our contact page does not send or store your information. When you choose “Prepare email,” it opens your own email app with your message addressed to us. We only receive your details if you send that email.

Emails you send us

When you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, company, email address, phone number and business details. We use it to reply to you, assess and manage a potential or existing business relationship, and keep records of our business correspondence.

Hosting and fonts

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are delivered by Google Fonts. To deliver the website, these providers receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details, which they handle under their own privacy policies.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where needed to run our business (for example, with our email provider), where you have asked us to, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep business correspondence for as long as needed for the relationship and for our legal, tax and accounting records.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent us by emailing info@aliyanllc.com. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed to children.

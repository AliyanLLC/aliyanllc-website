About ALIYAN LLC

A US wholesale buyer built for brand-safe selling.

ALIYAN LLC is a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Virginia. We buy authentic products wholesale and sell them to customers on Amazon.com.

Become a wholesale partnerContact us
Who we are

A reseller, not a distributor.

ALIYAN LLC was established in 2026 to buy products wholesale from brand owners and their authorized distributors, and to resell them to end customers on Amazon.com in the United States using Fulfillment by Amazon.

We are a reseller. We do not manufacture products, we do not sell to other businesses, and we do not export the products we buy. That focus is deliberate: it lets us protect the brands we carry and build relationships that last.

What we focus on

Four things, done properly.

Wholesale sourcing

Opening direct accounts with brand owners and their authorized distributors, and buying to their minimum order quantities.

Marketplace retail

Selling to end customers on Amazon.com in the United States, with Fulfillment by Amazon handling storage and delivery.

Brand protection

Respecting MAP and brand policies, selling on existing listings and reporting listing problems to the brand.

Long-term relationships

Fewer lines, represented properly, with consistent reorders and clear communication.

How we work

The principles behind every account.

A paper trail for every unit

Every product we sell can be traced to an invoice from the brand or an authorized distributor.

Your policies come first

MAP, reseller agreements and brand guidelines are part of how we sell, not an afterthought.

One marketplace, done carefully

We sell only on Amazon.com in the US. We do not add marketplaces without the brand’s approval.

Straight answers

Clear purchase orders, agreed terms and a single point of contact for each supplier.

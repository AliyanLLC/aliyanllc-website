What we do

A focused retail partner for one marketplace.

We concentrate on Amazon.com and do it carefully: buying the right quantities, protecting how your products appear, and letting Amazon's fulfillment network deliver to the customer.

Sourcing

Wholesale purchasing

We open direct accounts, buy to your minimum order quantities and pay on the terms we agree. Every unit we sell is backed by an invoice from you or your authorized distributor.

Listings

Listing care

We sell on your existing product pages and check that titles, images and details match your brand guidelines. If something on a listing is wrong, we report it to you instead of changing it ourselves.

Fulfillment

FBA and inventory planning

Stock ships to Amazon fulfillment centers, so customers get Prime delivery and Amazon's handling. We plan reorders from real sell-through to avoid both stock-outs and excess inventory.

Value for your brand

More than a buyer. A caretaker for your listings.

A wholesale account should be good for the brand as well as the reseller. On every product we carry, this is the work we do for you, at no cost to you.

Listing quality

Listing review and fixes

We review titles, images, bullet points and search terms on your listings and send you suggested improvements for your approval.

Brand Registry

A+ Content support

If you own your Brand Registry, we can draft A+ Content and Brand Store copy for you to review and publish.

Visibility

Advertising on your listings

We can run Sponsored Products campaigns on the listings we sell, at our own cost, to grow your visibility and sales on Amazon.

Protection

Listing watch

We watch your listings for counterfeit offers, listing hijacks and unauthorized sellers, and report what we find to you.

Availability

Always in stock

We keep your best sellers stocked at Amazon and plan reorders ahead of peak periods such as Prime Day and the fourth quarter.

Reporting

Monthly performance report

Units sold, stock on hand and customer feedback for your line, sent monthly or whenever you ask.

Complimentary

Free Amazon listing review for your brand

Send us your brand name. Within three business days we will email you a short, practical review of your Amazon listings with specific improvements. No commitment required.

Reseller Charter

Our written commitments to every brand and distributor we buy from.

  1. I

    Minimum advertised price

    We never advertise below your MAP and we do not undercut the Buy Box to win sales.

  2. II

    Authorized sourcing

    We buy only from brand owners and their authorized distributors, and keep every invoice on file.

  3. III

    No diversion

    We sell only to end customers on Amazon.com in the United States. We do not resell to other retailers or wholesalers, and we do not export your products.

  4. IV

    Listing integrity

    We sell on your existing listings and never create duplicates or change detail pages without your approval.

  5. V

    Product condition

    Every unit is new, sealed, in original packaging and prepared to Amazon's FBA requirements.

  6. VI

    Your instructions

    If you ask us to change how we sell your products, or to stop selling them, we act promptly.

  7. VII

    Account health

    We protect our seller metrics so your products stay available and well presented.

  8. VIII

    Communication

    One point of contact, and a reply within one business day.

Process

From first email to first order.

Opening an account with us follows your process. Here is what to expect.

  1. 1

    Introduction

    Tell us which lines you make or distribute. We reply with our company details and the products we would like to carry.

  2. 2

    Account approval

    We complete your credit application, reseller agreement and any MAP or brand policy you require.

  3. 3

    Opening order

    We place a first order at your minimum quantity, with ship-to instructions on the purchase order.

  4. 4

    Launch check

    Before stock goes live, we check each listing against your brand guidelines and flag anything that needs your attention.

  5. 5

    Reorders and reporting

    We reorder from sell-through and keep you informed about how your line is performing.

FAQ

Questions distributors ask us.

Straight answers to the questions that usually come up before a new account is approved.

Where will you sell our products?
Only on Amazon.com, to customers in the United States, fulfilled by Amazon. We do not add other marketplaces without your approval.
Will you sell below MAP?
No. We follow your MAP policy on every unit, including during promotions and peak seasons.
Do you resell to other businesses or export?
No. We sell only to end customers. We do not divert product to other resellers, wholesalers or foreign markets.
Will you create new listings for our products?
Only with your written approval. We normally sell on your existing ASINs.
What does an opening order look like?
We start at your minimum order quantity, then increase reorders as the product sells through.
What do you need from us to get started?
Your new-account or credit application and your price list. We complete the application with our company details and share our registration documents.

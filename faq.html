About ALIYAN LLC

What does ALIYAN LLC do?

ALIYAN LLC is a Virginia-registered company that buys products wholesale from brand owners and authorized distributors and resells them to end customers on Amazon.com in the United States, using Fulfillment by Amazon.

Do you offer wholesale products to other businesses?

No. ALIYAN LLC is a wholesale buyer, not a distributor. We buy from brand owners and authorized distributors and sell only to end customers on Amazon.com. We do not resell to other retailers or wholesalers.

Where will you sell our products?

Only on Amazon.com, to customers in the United States, fulfilled by Amazon. We do not add other marketplaces without your approval.

How can I contact ALIYAN LLC?

Email wholesale@aliyanllc.com for new accounts, price lists and partnership questions, or info@aliyanllc.com for general enquiries. You can also use the inquiry form on our contact page.

Working with us

How can I become a wholesale partner?

Send us your line sheet or catalog and your account requirements through the contact page or by email. We review the line, complete your new-account application and agree terms with you before the first order.

How can I request information about ALIYAN LLC?

Email wholesale@aliyanllc.com. We can share our company details, and company registration documents are available on request.

How are wholesale terms determined?

By your account requirements: your price list, minimum order quantity, payment terms and any MAP or reseller policy. We agree terms in writing before placing the first order.

Will you sell below MAP?

No. We follow your MAP policy on every unit, including during promotions and peak seasons.

Will you create new listings for our products?

Only with your written approval. We normally sell on your existing ASINs.

What does an opening order look like?

We start at your minimum order quantity, then increase reorders as the product sells through.

What do you need from us to get started?

Your new-account or credit application and your price list. We complete the application with our company details and share our registration documents on request.

How quickly do you reply?

We aim to reply to every supplier within one business day.

Products and categories

What product categories do you source?

Pet Supplies, Home & Kitchen, Household Essentials, Personal Care Accessories, Office Supplies and Tools & Hardware. Each category page lists the product types we are currently sourcing.

Do you provide product catalogs?

Our wholesale catalog lists the product types we want to buy. Because we are a buyer, we ask suppliers to send us their own catalog or line sheet.

Do you work with suppliers outside the USA?

Our sales channel is Amazon.com in the United States, so we focus on brands and authorized distributors that supply the US market. If you are based outside the USA, tell us about your US distribution and we will review it.

Do you export or divert products?

No. We do not export the products we buy or divert them to other resellers or foreign markets.
